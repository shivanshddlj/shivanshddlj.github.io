Research

Writing from my MSc at UCL

Technology strategy, adoption and resilience, applied to real organisations and infrastructure.

MSc dissertation · Aug 2025

Strategic Adoption of Industry-Leading AI Decarbonisation Technologies in India's Non-Renewable Energy Infrastructure

A multi-method qualitative study of what helps and blocks AI adoption for decarbonisation in India's non-renewable energy assets. It combines the TOE framework with UTAUT2 and finds the technology is high-impact and feasible, but scaling is held back by low maturity, legacy systems, thin digital infrastructure, AI skills gaps, cost and shifting regulation. The recommendations focus on stronger data foundations, regulatory incentives and using AI to build individual capability.
